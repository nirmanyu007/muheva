Good to Know

Shipping & Returns

Everything you need to know about how your order reaches you.

Home / Shipping

Processing Time

Every piece is prepared with care once an order is placed. Please allow 2–3 business days for your order to be packed and handed off for delivery.

Shipping Rates & Delivery

  • Free shipping on all domestic orders
  • Standard delivery: 4–7 business days within India
  • Express delivery available at checkout for select pin codes
  • International shipping available on request — reach out via our Contact page for rates

Order Tracking

Once your order ships, you'll receive a confirmation with tracking details so you can follow its journey to your door.

Returns & Exchanges

We currently do not accept returns unless the product received is defective or damaged. If you receive a defective item, reach out to us within 7 days of delivery through our Contact page and we'll arrange a replacement.

Exchanges (for size or item, where available) are accepted within 7 days of delivery, provided the item is unworn, unwashed, and in its original condition with tags attached. Please note that additional delivery charges apply for exchanges and will be borne by the customer.

Questions?

If you have any questions about an order, reach out any time through our Contact page — we're happy to help.
